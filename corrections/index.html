Corrections & contact

How to report an error, what evidence we check, and how to reach the editorial team.

What counts as a correction

Wrong runtime, a release date that does not match the studio listing, a cast member who is not in the film, a mirrored poster, a broken or unauthorised watch link, a certification that changed, or a provider that no longer carries the title. If a page states something the source does not support, that is a correction too.

How to report it

Send the page URL, the exact sentence or field, and the source that shows the right value. One message per issue is easiest to track. The fastest route is a message to our Facebook page, MovieState. You can also open an issue on the public tracker, github.com/moviestate/moviestate.github.io/issues, which keeps a public record of what was reported and how it was resolved.

What happens next

Reports are reviewed in the order they arrive, usually within a week. We check the claim against the TMDB record, then against the studio or provider listing. If the page is wrong we fix it and the page shows the new update date. If the listed value is supported by the source, we reply with the evidence instead of changing it. Substantive corrections are noted in the page text rather than silently overwritten.

What we will not do

We do not remove accurate information because a rights holder or publicist prefers a different framing. We do not add, remove or reorder a page in exchange for payment. We do not publish links to unlicensed streaming or download sites, and we remove any that slip through.

Data sources

Titles, cast, crew, runtimes, release dates, certifications, provider listings and community ratings come from TMDB. Images are served from the TMDB image CDN. Availability is checked against the provider listing before a watch link is published, and every provider link on a movie page points at a licensed service. See the editorial policy for how pages are written and reviewed.

Advertising and links

MovieState is not a streaming service and hosts no video. Some outbound links to licensed stores and streamers may earn a commission. That never changes the price you pay and never changes which services we list or the order they appear in.
