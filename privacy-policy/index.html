Privacy Policy

The short version

This site is a set of static pages. We do not create accounts, we do not ask for your name or email, and we do not run advertising networks or cross-site trackers.

What is collected

Hosting: this site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and requested URL in its own server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Images: posters and backdrops are loaded from TMDB's image service. That request carries the technical data any image request carries, such as your IP address and browser headers.

Analytics: we use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-TV11JN6V5Q) to count page views and see which guides are useful. GA4 sets first-party cookies and records a truncated IP address, device type, referrer and the pages you open. Google processes that data under its own privacy terms. You can block it with any content blocker or with Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on, and the site keeps working. We do not use analytics for advertising, we do not sell data, and we do not build profiles of individual readers.

Cookies

We do not set cookies for advertising or profiling. If you follow a link to another site, that site's own cookie and privacy rules apply.

Links to other sites

Movie pages link only to licensed services: a studio's own site, a licensed streamer or digital store that legitimately sells or streams the film, or a JustWatch comparison page. We do not link to unlicensed streaming, download or mirror sites. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content or data practices.

Children

This site is general-audience information about films and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the update will appear here with a new date. For privacy questions or removal requests, use the corrections and contact page. We read every message and reply where a reply is possible.
