Brandon Perea

Brandon Perea (born May 25, 1995) is an American actor and professional jam skater. He is best known for his performance as Alfonso "French" Sosa on The OA and his breakthrough role as Angel Torres in Jordan Peele's 2022 science fiction horror film Nope.
