The basics

About the guide

What is MovieState?

MovieState is an independent movie guide that explains what a film is, who made it, and where you can watch it.

Is MovieState free to use?

Yes. Every page is free to read. There is no account, no paywall and no forced sign-up.

Do you host or stream the movies?

No. We publish information only. Playback and download pages are handled on our main site, moviestate.is-best.net.

Where does your movie data come from?

Core metadata and artwork come from TMDB. We check release details and availability against official announcements before publishing.

How often are pages updated?

We refresh the library continuously, and we prioritise pages where a release date, runtime or streaming status has changed.

Why can availability differ from what I see?

Streaming rights are sold country by country and change over time. A film available in one region may not be licensed in another.

Availability and playback

Can I request a movie page?

Yes. Send us the title through our main site's contact options and we will add it to the research queue.

Do you publish free download promises?

No. Claims like 'free full movie download' are usually false, and we do not publish them. We link only to pages that explain availability honestly.

How do you handle errors?

Report the page and the detail that is wrong. We verify it against a primary source and correct the page, noting significant changes.

Can I use your text on my site?

Short quotations with a link are fine. Republishing full pages is not permitted.
