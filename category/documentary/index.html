How to choose

What to look for in documentary films

Documentaries turn research into narrative. The best ones earn trust through verifiable sourcing, give subjects room to speak, and resist the urge to flatten complicated evidence into a tidy conclusion.

When documentary guides arrive, we will list the filmmaker, the sources used and whether the film has been disputed.

Where to start: Until then, browse the full movie library or the drama and history categories.
