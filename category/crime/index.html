How to choose

What to look for in crime films

Crime stories trade in procedure, motive and consequence. The genre ranges from heist precision to slow-burn character studies, and the strongest entries keep the tension in the choices rather than the body count.

Ask what the film is about besides the crime. Great entries are really about corruption, loyalty or a city. Check the writer and director: crime films are among the most writer-driven in cinema.

Where to start: Start with the one that has the strongest ensemble cast. Crime stories live in the supporting roles.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best crime movies here?

The Dark Knight and Collision Course are the highest rated crime guides here. Ratings are TMDB user scores and change over time.

What separates a crime film from a thriller?

Crime centres on the act and its investigation or consequences. Thrillers centre on the threat and keeping you uneasy. Many films here are both, which is why they appear in several categories.

Are crime films violent?

Often, but not always graphically. The certification on each film page reflects the level, so check it before a family viewing.
