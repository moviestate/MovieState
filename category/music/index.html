How to choose

What to look for in music films

Music films live on performance. Concert footage, biopics and musicals all depend on how honestly the camera captures a voice, and on whether the soundtrack earns its place in the story.

Check how much of the music is live or newly recorded, and who composed the score. The composer is listed in the crew section of each film page.

Where to start: Choose by the music you already like. A great soundtrack for your taste lifts a good film.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best music movies here?

Michael, Whiplash and Coco are the highest rated music guides here. Ratings are TMDB user scores and change over time.

Are music films the same as musicals?

No. A musical has characters sing within the story. A music film can be a biopic, concert or documentary about musicians.

Where can I hear the soundtrack?

The composer and music credits are in the crew section of each film page, which is the best starting point for finding the official release.
