How to choose

What to look for in western films

The western is a study of frontier rules: land, law, violence and who gets to start over. Modern entries often invert the classics, but the genre still trades on wide landscapes and hard choices.

When guides arrive, we will note whether each film follows or subverts the classic form.

Where to start: Browse the adventure and drama categories in the meantime.
