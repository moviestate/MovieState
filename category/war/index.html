How to choose

What to look for in war films

War films carry a documentary weight even when fictional. The strongest avoid triumphalism, focus on the people inside the machinery, and are honest about how little control individuals have.

Check whether the story follows a real event, and treat the film as interpretation. The strongest war films are honest about how little control individuals have.

Where to start: If you are new to the genre, pick the one with the shortest runtime. These films take a lot out of you.
