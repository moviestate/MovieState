Understanding the genre

What makes a good mystery film

Mystery rewards attention. Clues, alibis and unreliable narrators are the toolkit, and the genre's pleasure is the gap between what the audience knows and what the detective can prove.

This page keeps two kinds of titles side by side: films with a full MovieState guide, where you can read the cast, crew, runtime, release dates and regional availability, and popular titles in the same genre that are being searched right now.

Availability is the part most lists ignore. A film can be streamable in one country and only rentable in another, so every guide separates streaming, rental and purchase by region instead of promising a link that does not exist.
