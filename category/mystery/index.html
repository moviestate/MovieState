How to choose

What to look for in mystery films

Mystery rewards attention. Clues, alibis and unreliable narrators are the toolkit, and the genre's pleasure is the gap between what the audience knows and what the detective can prove.

The best mysteries play fair: every clue is on screen before the reveal. Check the writer's other work, because a strong plotter usually repeats the trick.

Where to start: Watch with the sound up and the phone away. These are the films where a missed line is a missed clue.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best mystery movies here?

Se7en, Psycho and High and Low are the highest rated mystery guides here. Ratings are TMDB user scores and change over time.

Do mystery films have spoilers on these pages?

No. Our overviews describe the setup only and never the resolution.

How do mysteries differ from thrillers?

Mysteries are about finding out what happened. Thrillers are about what will happen next. Many films blend the two.
