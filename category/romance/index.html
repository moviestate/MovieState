How to choose

What to look for in romance films

Romance depends on chemistry and structure: two people, an obstacle, and a reason to keep watching after the meet-cute. The genre's range runs from sweeping period drama to small, sharp modern stories.

Judge the leads first. A romance without chemistry is a failed film regardless of production value. Then check the tone: warm comedy, period drama and tragedy are different evenings.

Where to start: Choose by mood. Pick comedy for a light night and drama for one you want to feel.
