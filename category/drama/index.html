How to choose

What to look for in drama films

Drama is the widest category here: family stories, courtroom battles, quiet character pieces. What holds it together is performance-driven writing, where small decisions carry the weight normally given to plot twists.

Dramas rise or fall on casting and script. Check who wrote it, and see whether the lead has done comparable work, because the person page links their other films.

Where to start: If you want something light, choose the shortest. If you want to be moved, choose the highest rated and set aside the evening.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best drama movies here?

The Shawshank Redemption, The Godfather and Schindler's List are the highest rated drama guides here. Ratings are TMDB user scores and change over time.

Why are some dramas rated lower than action films?

Drama audiences are smaller and more divided, and slow pacing costs scores. A 7 for a drama is often a strong result.

How long are drama films?

Longer than average, often two hours or more. The runtime is on every film page so you can plan.
