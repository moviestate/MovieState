How to choose

What to look for in thriller films

Thrillers are engineered for tension: a clock, a threat and someone with too little information. Pacing does most of the work, which is why editing and sound design matter as much as the plot.

Editing and sound design matter as much as the plot. Check the runtime: a thriller over two hours must keep raising the stakes to hold you.

Where to start: Pick the highest rated for a safe evening. Pick the one with the least familiar cast if you want to be surprised.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best thriller movies here?

The Dark Knight, Pulp Fiction and Parasite are the highest rated thriller guides here. Ratings are TMDB user scores and change over time.

Why are so many films listed as thrillers?

The label is broad. It covers horror, crime, action and mystery titles that rely on suspense, so many films carry it alongside another genre.

Are thrillers scary?

Some are, but suspense is different from fear. Horror aims to frighten. Thrillers aim to make you anxious about what happens next.
