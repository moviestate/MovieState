How to choose

What to look for in family films

Family films have to work for two audiences at once — children following the action and adults reading the jokes underneath. The genre's best entries respect both, which is why they stay in rotation for years.

Check the certification and the themes. A G or PG label does not mean a film is gentle, and some animated titles deal with loss.

Where to start: Choose by age of the youngest viewer, then by runtime. Under 100 minutes is the safe range for younger children.
