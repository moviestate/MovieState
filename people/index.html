How profiles work

Credits first, biography second

Every profile lists the films that person worked on inside our library, with the character or crew role they covered. Biographies are sourced from TMDB and shortened where needed; where no biography exists we publish credits only rather than filler text.

Why credits matter

Follow the people, not just the title

Most viewers pick a film by title. A smaller, happier group picks by person: the director whose last three films landed, the actor who turns a thin script into something watchable, the cinematographer whose work you recognise before you know why. This index exists for that second group.

Each profile lists every film we cover in which that person appears or directs, with the character name where the film's credits confirm it, plus the ratings and runtime for a quick reality check. Where a biography is available, we include it so you can place the person's career without opening five other tabs.

Profiles are built from live production data and updated as new titles are added to the library. If a credit is missing, it is almost always because the film itself is not yet covered — the person page fills in as the movie library grows.
