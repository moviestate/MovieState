The story

What Kraken is about

A marine biologist is doing research on a fish farm when she encounters several strange occurrences. Along with the brutal deaths of two teenagers, all signs point to the deep fjord; can there be more to the depths than the eye can see?

Kraken is a horror, action and thriller film from 2026, directed by Pål Øie. It runs 1h 34m. It was produced by Handmade films in Norwegian Woods and Nordisk Film Norway, with production in Norway.

The main cast is led by Sara Khorami, Mikkel Bratt Silset, Ingvild Holthe Bygdnes, Øyvind Brandtzæg. The screenplay was written by Vilde Eide, Kjersti Helen Rasmussen. Reported worldwide gross stands at $14.3M.
