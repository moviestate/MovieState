Should you watch Princess Mononoke?

Princess Mononoke (1997) is best described as adventure. Ashitaka, a prince of the disappearing Emishi people, is cursed by a demonized boar god and must journey to the west to find a cure. Along the way, he encounters San, a young human woman fighting to protect the forest, and Lady Eboshi, who is trying to destroy it. Ashitaka must find a way to bring balance to this conflict.

Directed by Hayao Miyazaki. Lead cast: Yoji Matsuda, Yuriko Ishida, Yuko Tanaka.
