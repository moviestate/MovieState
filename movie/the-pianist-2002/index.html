The story

What The Pianist is about

The true story of pianist Władysław Szpilman's experiences in Warsaw during the Nazi occupation. When the Jews of the city find themselves forced into a ghetto, Szpilman finds work playing in a café; and when his family is deported in 1942, he stays behind, works for a while as a laborer, and eventually goes into hiding in the ruins of the war-torn city.

The Pianist is a drama and war film from 2002, directed by Roman Polanski. It runs 2h 30m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by R.P. Productions, Heritage Films and Studio Babelsberg, with production in France, Germany, Poland.

The main cast is led by Adrien Brody, Thomas Kretschmann, Frank Finlay, Maureen Lipman. The screenplay was written by Ronald Harwood. Reported worldwide gross stands at $120.1M.
