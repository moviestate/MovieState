What Oppenheimer is about

The story of J. Robert Oppenheimer's role in the development of the atomic bomb during World War II.

Oppenheimer is a drama, history and war film from 2023, directed by Christopher Nolan. It runs 3h 01m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Syncopy, Universal Pictures and Atlas Entertainment, with production in United Kingdom, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Cillian Murphy, Emily Blunt, Matt Damon, Robert Downey Jr.. The screenplay was written by Christopher Nolan. Reported worldwide gross stands at $952.0M.
