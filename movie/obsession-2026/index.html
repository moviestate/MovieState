What Obsession is about

After breaking the mysterious "One Wish Willow" to win his crush's heart, a hopeless romantic finds himself getting exactly what he asked for but soon discovers that some desires come at a dark, sinister price.

Obsession is a horror and thriller film from 2026, directed by Curry Barker. It runs 1h 49m. It was produced by Tea Shop Productions, Under the Shell and Capstone Pictures, with production in United States of America, United Kingdom.

The main cast is led by Michael Johnston, Inde Navarrette, Cooper Tomlinson, Megan Lawless. The screenplay was written by Curry Barker. Reported worldwide gross stands at $525.1M.
