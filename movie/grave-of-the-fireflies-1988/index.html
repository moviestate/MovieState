The story

What Grave of the Fireflies is about

In the final months of World War II, 14-year-old Seita and his sister Setsuko are orphaned when their mother is killed during an air raid in Kobe, Japan. After a falling out with their aunt, they move into an abandoned bomb shelter. With no surviving relatives and their emergency rations depleted, Seita and Setsuko struggle to survive.

Grave of the Fireflies is an animation, drama and war film from 1988, directed by Isao Takahata. It runs 1h 29m and is rated NR in the US. It was produced by Studio Ghibli, with production in Japan.

The main cast is led by Tsutomu Tatsumi, Ayano Shiraishi, Yoshiko Shinohara, Akemi Yamaguchi. The screenplay was written by Isao Takahata. Reported worldwide gross stands at $5.0M.
