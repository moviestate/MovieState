The story

What Se7en is about

Two homicide detectives are on a desperate hunt for a serial killer whose crimes are based on the "seven deadly sins" in this dark and haunting film that takes viewers from the tortured remains of one victim to the next. The seasoned Det. Somerset researches each sin in an effort to get inside the killer's mind, while his novice partner, Mills, scoffs at his efforts to unravel the case.

Se7en is a crime, mystery and thriller film from 1995, directed by David Fincher. It runs 2h 07m. It was produced by New Line Cinema, Juno Pix and Arnold Kopelson Productions, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Morgan Freeman, Brad Pitt, Gwyneth Paltrow, John Cassini. The screenplay was written by Andrew Kevin Walker. Reported worldwide gross stands at $327.3M.
