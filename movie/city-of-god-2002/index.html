The story

What City of God is about

In the poverty-stricken favelas of Rio de Janeiro in the 1970s, two young men choose different paths. Rocket is a budding photographer who documents the increasing drug-related violence of his neighborhood, while José “Zé” Pequeno is an ambitious drug dealer diving into a dangerous life of crime.

City of God is a drama and crime film from 2002, directed by Fernando Meirelles. It runs 2h 09m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by O2 Filmes, VideoFilmes and Wild Bunch, with production in Brazil, France, Germany.

The main cast is led by Alexandre Rodrigues, Leandro Firmino, Phellipe Haagensen, Douglas Silva. The screenplay was written by Bráulio Mantovani. Reported worldwide gross stands at $30.6M.
