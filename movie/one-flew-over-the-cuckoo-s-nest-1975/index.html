The story

What One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest is about

A petty criminal fakes insanity to serve his sentence in a mental ward rather than prison. He soon finds himself as a leader to the other patients—and an enemy to the cruel, domineering nurse who runs the ward.

One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest is a drama film from 1975, directed by Miloš Forman. It runs 2h 15m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Fantasy Films, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Jack Nicholson, Brad Dourif, Louise Fletcher, Danny DeVito. The screenplay was written by Bo Goldman, Lawrence Hauben. Reported worldwide gross stands at $109.0M.
