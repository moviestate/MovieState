The story

What The Shawshank Redemption is about

Imprisoned in the 1940s for the double murder of his wife and her lover, upstanding banker Andy Dufresne begins a new life at the Shawshank prison, where he puts his accounting skills to work for an amoral warden. During his long stretch in prison, Dufresne comes to be admired by the other inmates -- including an older prisoner named Red -- for his integrity and unquenchable sense of hope.

The Shawshank Redemption is a drama and crime film from 1994, directed by Frank Darabont. It runs 2h 22m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Castle Rock Entertainment, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Tim Robbins, Morgan Freeman, Bob Gunton, William Sadler. The screenplay was written by Frank Darabont. Reported worldwide gross stands at $28.3M.
