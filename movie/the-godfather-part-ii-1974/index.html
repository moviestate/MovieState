The story

What The Godfather Part II is about

In the continuing saga of the Corleone crime family, a young Vito Corleone grows up in Sicily and in 1910s New York. In the 1950s, Michael Corleone attempts to expand the family business into Las Vegas, Hollywood and Cuba.

The Godfather Part II is a drama and crime film from 1974, directed by Francis Ford Coppola. It runs 3h 22m. It was produced by Paramount Pictures, The Coppola Company and American Zoetrope, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Al Pacino, Robert Duvall, Diane Keaton, Robert De Niro. The screenplay was written by Mario Puzo, Francis Ford Coppola. Reported worldwide gross stands at $102.6M.
