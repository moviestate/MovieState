The story

What Once Upon a Time in America is about

A former Prohibition-era Jewish gangster returns to the Lower East Side of Manhattan over thirty years later, where he once again must confront the ghosts and regrets of his old life.

Once Upon a Time in America is a drama and crime film from 1984, directed by Sergio Leone. It runs 2h 19m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Embassy International Pictures, PSO and The Ladd Company, with production in Italy, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Robert De Niro, James Woods, Elizabeth McGovern, Joe Pesci. The screenplay was written by Sergio Leone, Franco Arcalli. Reported worldwide gross stands at $5.5M.
