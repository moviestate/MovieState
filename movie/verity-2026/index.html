The story

What Verity is about

Lowen Ashleigh, hired to ghostwrite for best-selling author Verity Crawford, uncovers Verity's autobiographical notes, and becomes involved in what may prove to be a lethal conspiracy.

Verity is a mystery and thriller film from 2026, directed by Michael Showalter. It runs 1h 57m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Eat the Cat, Semi-Formal Productions and Somewhere Pictures, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Dakota Johnson, Anne Hathaway, Josh Hartnett, Brady Wagner. The screenplay was written by Nick Antosca. Reported worldwide gross stands at $163.7M.
