The story

What Seven Samurai is about

A samurai answers a village's request for protection after he falls on hard times. The town needs protection from bandits, so the samurai gathers six others to help him teach the people how to defend themselves, and the villagers provide the soldiers with food.

Seven Samurai is an action and drama film from 1954, directed by Akira Kurosawa. It runs 3h 27m and is rated NR in the US. It was produced by TOHO, with production in Japan.

The main cast is led by Toshirō Mifune, Takashi Shimura, Yoshio Inaba, Seiji Miyaguchi. The screenplay was written by Hideo Oguni, Shinobu Hashimoto. Reported worldwide gross stands at $105.0M.
