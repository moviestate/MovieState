The story

What Life Is Beautiful is about

A touching story of an Italian book seller of Jewish ancestry who lives in his own little fairy tale. His creative and happy life would come to an abrupt halt when his entire family is deported to a concentration camp during World War II. While locked up he tries to convince his son that the whole thing is just a game.

Life Is Beautiful is a comedy and drama film from 1997, directed by Roberto Benigni. It runs 1h 56m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Mario e Vittorio Cecchi Gori - C.E.I.A.D. and Melampo Cinematografica, with production in Italy.

The main cast is led by Roberto Benigni, Nicoletta Braschi, Giorgio Cantarini, Giustino Durano. The screenplay was written by Roberto Benigni, Vincenzo Cerami. Reported worldwide gross stands at $230.1M.
