The story

What 12 Angry Men is about

The defense and the prosecution have rested and the jury is filing into the jury room to decide if a young Spanish-American is guilty or innocent of murdering his father. What begins as an open and shut case soon becomes a mini-drama of each of the jurors' prejudices and preconceptions about the trial, the accused, and each other.

12 Angry Men is a drama film from 1957, directed by Sidney Lumet. It runs 1h 37m and is rated NR in the US. It was produced by Orion-Nova Productions, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Martin Balsam, John Fiedler, Lee J. Cobb, E.G. Marshall. The screenplay was written by Reginald Rose. Reported worldwide gross stands at $4.4M.
