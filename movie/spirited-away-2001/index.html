The story

What Spirited Away is about

A young girl, Chihiro, becomes trapped in a strange new world of spirits. When her parents undergo a mysterious transformation, she must call upon the courage she never knew she had to free her family.

Spirited Away is an animation, family and fantasy film from 2001, directed by Hayao Miyazaki. It runs 2h 05m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Studio Ghibli, Tokuma Shoten and Nippon Television Network Corporation, with production in Japan.

The main cast is led by Rumi Hiiragi, Miyu Irino, Mari Natsuki, Takashi Naito. The screenplay was written by Hayao Miyazaki. Reported worldwide gross stands at $274.9M.
