What Minions & Monsters is about

This is the rambunctious, ridiculous and totally true story of how the Minions conquered Hollywood, became movie stars, lost everything, unleashed monsters onto the world and then banded together to try and save the planet from the mayhem they had just created.

Minions & Monsters is an adventure, animation, comedy, family and fantasy film from 2026, directed by Pierre Coffin. It runs 1h 30m. It was produced by Universal Pictures and Illumination, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Pierre Coffin, Trey Parker, Christoph Waltz, Allison Janney. The screenplay was written by Brian Lynch, Pierre Coffin. Reported worldwide gross stands at $526.3M.
