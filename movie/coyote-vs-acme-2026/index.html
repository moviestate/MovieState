What Coyote vs. Acme is about

After Acme products fail him one too many times in his dogged pursuit of the Roadrunner, Wile E. Coyote decides to hire a billboard lawyer to sue the Acme Corporation.

Coyote vs. Acme is a comedy, adventure, family and animation film from 2026, directed by Dave Green. It runs 1h 43m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Troll Court Entertainment, Keylight Pictures and Warner Animation Group, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Will Forte, Lana Condor, John Cena, Tone Bell. The screenplay was written by Samy Burch. Reported worldwide gross stands at $101.5M.
