What Deadpool & Wolverine is about

A listless Wade Wilson toils away in civilian life with his days as the morally flexible mercenary, Deadpool, behind him. But when his homeworld faces an existential threat, Wade must reluctantly suit-up again with an even more reluctant Wolverine.

Deadpool & Wolverine is an action, comedy and science fiction film from 2024, directed by Shawn Levy. It runs 2h 08m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Marvel Studios, Maximum Effort and 21 Laps Entertainment, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Ryan Reynolds, Hugh Jackman, Emma Corrin, Matthew Macfadyen. The screenplay was written by Rhett Reese, Paul Wernick. Reported worldwide gross stands at $1338.1M.
