The story

What Buddy is about

Inside the colorful world of "It's Buddy!", a group of children spend their days singing, dancing, and helping Buddy - bright orange unicorn - spread happiness. But when one child refuses to play along, Buddy is not pleased and cracks begin to appear in this seemingly perfect world.

Buddy is a horror, comedy and fantasy film from 2026, directed by Casper Kelly. It runs 1h 36m. It was produced by BoulderLight Pictures, Low Spark Films and SIPUR, with production in United States of America, Israel.

The main cast is led by Keegan-Michael Key, Delaney Quinn, Tristan Borders, Madison Skyy Polan. The screenplay was written by Casper Kelly, Jamie King. Reported worldwide gross stands at $27.0M.
