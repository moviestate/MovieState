What Runner is about

A former soldier is thrown into a brutal race against time when a critical medical delivery makes him and his unlikely partner the targets of a ruthless cartel. As the mission spirals into a deadly manhunt, every detour, delay, and bullet pushes them closer to failure. With betrayal closing in from every side, they must survive the chase and deliver their cargo to save the life of a little girl.

Runner is an action and comedy film from 2026, directed by Scott Waugh. It runs 1h 37m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Broken Road Productions, AllyCat Entertainment and Nickel City Pictures, with production in United States of America, Australia, United Kingdom.

The main cast is led by Alan Ritchson, Owen Wilson, Rodrigo Santoro, Leila George. The screenplay was written by Tommy White, Miles Hubley. Reported worldwide gross stands at $18.1M.
