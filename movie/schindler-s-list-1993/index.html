The story

What Schindler's List is about

The true story of how businessman Oskar Schindler saved over a thousand Jewish lives from the Nazis while they worked as slaves in his factory during World War II.

Schindler's List is a drama, history and war film from 1993, directed by Steven Spielberg. It runs 3h 15m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Amblin Entertainment, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Liam Neeson, Ben Kingsley, Ralph Fiennes, Caroline Goodall. The screenplay was written by Steven Zaillian. Reported worldwide gross stands at $321.4M.
