The story

What A Dog's Will is about

The lively João Grilo and the sly Chicó are poor guys living in the hinterland who cheat a bunch of people in a small town in Northeastern Brazil. When they die, they have to be judged by Christ, the Devil and the Virgin Mary before they are admitted to paradise.

A Dog's Will is a comedy, drama, fantasy and romance film from 2000, directed by Guel Arraes. It runs 1h 42m. It was produced by Lereby Produções, Globo Filmes and Estúdios Globo, with production in Brazil.

The main cast is led by Matheus Nachtergaele, Selton Mello, Fernanda Montenegro, Rogério Cardoso. The screenplay was written by Guel Arraes, Adriana Falcão. Reported worldwide gross stands at $4.9M.
