The story

What Forrest Gump is about

A man with a low IQ has accomplished great things in his life and been present during significant historic events—in each case, far exceeding what anyone imagined he could do. But despite all he has achieved, his one true love eludes him.

Forrest Gump is a comedy, drama and romance film from 1994, directed by Robert Zemeckis. It runs 2h 22m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Paramount Pictures, The Steve Tisch Company and Wendy Finerman Productions, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Tom Hanks, Robin Wright, Gary Sinise, Sally Field. The screenplay was written by Eric Roth. Reported worldwide gross stands at $677.4M.
