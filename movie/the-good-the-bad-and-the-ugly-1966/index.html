What The Good, the Bad and the Ugly is about

While the Civil War rages on between the Union and the Confederacy, three men – a quiet loner, a ruthless hitman, and a Mexican bandit – comb the American Southwest in search of a strongbox containing $200,000 in stolen gold.

The Good, the Bad and the Ugly is a western film from 1966, directed by Sergio Leone. It runs 2h 41m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by United Artists, PEA and Arturo González PC, with production in United States of America, Italy, Spain.

The main cast is led by Clint Eastwood, Eli Wallach, Lee Van Cleef, Aldo Giuffrè. The screenplay was written by Luciano Vincenzoni, Sergio Leone. Reported worldwide gross stands at $38.9M.
