What High and Low is about

A Yokohama shoe executive faces a wrenching choice when kidnappers mistakenly seize his chauffeur’s son but demand the ransom anyway.

High and Low is a drama, crime, thriller and mystery film from 1963, directed by Akira Kurosawa. It runs 2h 22m and is rated NR in the US. It was produced by TOHO and Kurosawa Production, with production in Japan.

The main cast is led by Toshirō Mifune, Tatsuya Nakadai, Kyōko Kagawa, Tatsuya Mihashi. The screenplay was written by Akira Kurosawa, Ryuzo Kikushima. Reported worldwide gross stands at an amount that has not been disclosed.
