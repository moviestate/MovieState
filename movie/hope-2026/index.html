The story

What Hope is about

In the remote South Korean village of Hope Harbor, police chief Bum-seok and officer Sung-ae are called to investigate a mysterious creature wreaking havoc on the community. But all is not as it seems, and perceptions can be misleading. What begins as ignorance plants the seed of disaster, escalating through human conflict into a tragedy of cosmic proportions.

Hope is a science fiction, mystery and action film from 2026, directed by Na Hong-jin. It runs 2h 37m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Forged Films, Plus M Entertainment and Westworld Story, with production in South Korea.

The main cast is led by Hwang Jung-min, Zo In-sung, Hoyeon, Alicia Vikander. The screenplay was written by Na Hong-jin. Reported worldwide gross stands at $49.5M.
