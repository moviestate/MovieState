The story

What Coraline is about

Wandering her rambling old house in her boring new town, 11-year-old Coraline discovers a hidden door to a strangely idealized version of her life. In order to stay in the fantasy, she must make a frighteningly real sacrifice.

Coraline is an animation, family, fantasy and horror film from 2009, directed by Henry Selick. It runs 1h 40m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by LAIKA and Pandemonium, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Dakota Fanning, Teri Hatcher, Jennifer Saunders, Dawn French. The screenplay was written by Henry Selick. Reported worldwide gross stands at $185.9M.
