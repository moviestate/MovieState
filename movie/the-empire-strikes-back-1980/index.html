The story

What The Empire Strikes Back is about

The epic saga continues as Luke Skywalker, in hopes of defeating the evil Galactic Empire, learns the ways of the Jedi from aging master Yoda. But Darth Vader is more determined than ever to capture Luke. Meanwhile, rebel leader Princess Leia, cocky Han Solo, Chewbacca, and droids C-3PO and R2-D2 are thrown into various stages of capture, betrayal and despair.

The Empire Strikes Back is an adventure, action and science fiction film from 1980, directed by Irvin Kershner. It runs 2h 04m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Lucasfilm Ltd., with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Mark Hamill, Harrison Ford, Carrie Fisher, Billy Dee Williams. The screenplay was written by Leigh Brackett, Lawrence Kasdan. Reported worldwide gross stands at $538.4M.
