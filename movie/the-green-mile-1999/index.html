The story

What The Green Mile is about

A supernatural tale set on death row in a Southern prison, where gentle giant John Coffey possesses the mysterious power to heal people's ailments. When the cell block's head guard, Paul Edgecomb, recognizes Coffey's miraculous gift, he tries desperately to help stave off the condemned man's execution.

The Green Mile is a fantasy, drama and crime film from 1999, directed by Frank Darabont. It runs 3h 09m. It was produced by Castle Rock Entertainment and Darkwoods Productions, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Tom Hanks, David Morse, Bonnie Hunt, Michael Clarke Duncan. The screenplay was written by Frank Darabont. Reported worldwide gross stands at $286.8M.
