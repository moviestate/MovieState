What Insidious: Out of the Further is about

Gemma, a young mother raising her daughter in the house she grew up in, discovers she can travel into The Further, where she possesses an ability to bring what lives there back to the real world.

Insidious: Out of the Further is a horror and thriller film from 2026, directed by Jacob Chase. It runs 1h 46m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Screen Gems, Stage 6 Films and Atomic Monster, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Amelia Eve, Island Austin, Brandon Perea, Lin Shaye. The screenplay was written by David Leslie Johnson-McGoldrick, Jacob Chase. Reported worldwide gross stands at $171.5M.
