What The End of Oak Street is about

After a mysterious cosmic event rips Oak Street from suburbia and transports their neighborhood to someplace unknown, the Platt family soon discovers that their very survival depends on them sticking together as they navigate their now unrecognizable surroundings.

The End of Oak Street is a science fiction, mystery and thriller film from 2026, directed by David Robert Mitchell. It runs 1h 40m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Warner Bros. Pictures, Domain Entertainment and Bad Robot, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Anne Hathaway, Ewan McGregor, Maisy Stella, Christian Convery. The screenplay was written by David Robert Mitchell. Reported worldwide gross stands at $120.4M.
