The story

What Inception is about

Cobb, a skilled thief who commits corporate espionage by infiltrating the subconscious of his targets is offered a chance to regain his old life as payment for a task considered to be impossible: "inception", the implantation of another person's idea into a target's subconscious.

Inception is an action, science fiction and crime film from 2010, directed by Christopher Nolan. It runs 2h 28m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Warner Bros. Pictures, Legendary Pictures and Syncopy, with production in United Kingdom, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Leonardo DiCaprio, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Ken Watanabe, Tom Hardy. The screenplay was written by Christopher Nolan. Reported worldwide gross stands at $839.0M.
