The story

What Better Off Dead... is about

High school student Lane Meyer sinks into suicidal depression when his girlfriend dumps him for jock Roy Stalin, the high school ski racing champion. Meanwhile, he has to deal with his eccentric family, a tenacious paperboy and an obnoxious neighbor whose mother is hosting a beautiful French exchange student named Monique.

Better Off Dead... is a comedy and romance film from 1985, directed by Savage Steve Holland. It runs 1h 37m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by A&M Films and CBS Theatrical Films, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by John Cusack, Amanda Wyss, Diane Franklin, Aaron Dozier. The screenplay was written by Savage Steve Holland. Reported worldwide gross stands at $10.3M.
