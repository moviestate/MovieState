What The Angry Birds Movie is about

An island populated entirely by happy, flightless birds or almost entirely. In this paradise, Red, a bird with a temper problem, speedy Chuck, and the volatile Bomb have always been outsiders. But when the island is visited by mysterious green piggies, it’s up to these unlikely outcasts to figure out what the pigs are up to.

The Angry Birds Movie is an animation, adventure, comedy and family film from 2016, directed by Clay Kaytis. It runs 1h 37m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Rovio Animation and Columbia Pictures, with production in Finland, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Jason Sudeikis, Josh Gad, Danny McBride, Maya Rudolph. The screenplay was written by Jon Vitti, John Cohen. Reported worldwide gross stands at $349.8M.
