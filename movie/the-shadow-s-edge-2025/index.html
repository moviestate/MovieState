What The Shadow's Edge is about

Macau Police brings the tracking expert police officer out of retirement to help catch a dangerous group of professional thieves.

The Shadow's Edge is an action, crime, drama and thriller film from 2025, directed by Larry Yang. It runs 2h 22m. It was produced by iQIYI Pictures （重复 同 iQiyi Motion Pictures）, Tao Piao Piao 淘票票 and Hairun Pictures Company, with production in China, Hong Kong.

The main cast is led by Jackie Chan, Zhang Zifeng, Tony Leung Ka-fai, Ci Sha. The screenplay was written by Larry Yang. Reported worldwide gross stands at $174.4M.
