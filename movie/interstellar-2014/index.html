The story

What Interstellar is about

The adventures of a group of explorers who make use of a newly discovered wormhole to surpass the limitations on human space travel and conquer the vast distances involved in an interstellar voyage.

Interstellar is an adventure, drama and science fiction film from 2014, directed by Christopher Nolan. It runs 2h 49m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Legendary Pictures, Syncopy and Lynda Obst Productions, with production in United Kingdom, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Matthew McConaughey, Anne Hathaway, Michael Caine, Jessica Chastain. The screenplay was written by Jonathan Nolan, Christopher Nolan. Reported worldwide gross stands at $746.6M.
