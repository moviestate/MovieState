The story

What Psycho is about

When larcenous real estate clerk Marion Crane goes on the lam with a wad of cash and hopes of starting a new life, she ends up at the notorious Bates Motel, where manager Norman Bates cares for his housebound mother.

Psycho is a horror, thriller and mystery film from 1960, directed by Alfred Hitchcock. It runs 1h 49m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Shamley Productions, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Anthony Perkins, Janet Leigh, Vera Miles, John Gavin. The screenplay was written by Joseph Stefano. Reported worldwide gross stands at $50.0M.
