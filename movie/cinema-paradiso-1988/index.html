The story

What Cinema Paradiso is about

A filmmaker recalls his childhood, when he fell in love with the movies at his village's theater and formed a deep friendship with the theater's projectionist.

Cinema Paradiso is a drama and romance film from 1988, directed by Giuseppe Tornatore. It runs 2h 04m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Cristaldifilm, Les Films Ariane and Rai 3, with production in Italy, France.

The main cast is led by Philippe Noiret, Jacques Perrin, Marco Leonardi, Salvatore Cascio. The screenplay was written by Giuseppe Tornatore. Reported worldwide gross stands at $36.0M.
