The story

What The Nightmare Before Christmas is about

Tired of scaring humans every October 31 with the same old bag of tricks, Jack Skellington, the spindly king of Halloween Town, kidnaps Santa Claus and plans to deliver shrunken heads and other ghoulish gifts to children on Christmas morning. But as Christmas approaches, Jack's rag-doll girlfriend, Sally, tries to foil his misguided plans.

The Nightmare Before Christmas is a fantasy, animation and family film from 1993, directed by Henry Selick. It runs 1h 16m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Touchstone Pictures and Skellington Productions, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Danny Elfman, Chris Sarandon, Catherine O'Hara, William Hickey. The screenplay was written by Caroline Thompson, Tim Burton. Reported worldwide gross stands at $75.6M.
