The story

What Your Name. is about

High schoolers Mitsuha and Taki are complete strangers living separate lives. But one night, they suddenly switch places. Mitsuha wakes up in Taki’s body, and he in hers. This bizarre occurrence continues to happen randomly, and the two must adjust their lives around each other.

Your Name. is an animation, romance and drama film from 2016, directed by Makoto Shinkai. It runs 1h 46m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by CoMix Wave Films, TOHO and KADOKAWA, with production in Japan.

The main cast is led by Ryunosuke Kamiki, Mone Kamishiraishi, Ryo Narita, Aoi Yuki. The screenplay was written by Makoto Shinkai. Reported worldwide gross stands at $407.2M.
