What Avengers: Endgame is about

After the devastating events of Avengers: Infinity War, the universe is in ruins due to the efforts of the Mad Titan, Thanos. With the help of remaining allies, the Avengers must assemble once more in order to undo Thanos' actions and restore order to the universe once and for all, no matter what consequences may be in store.

Avengers: Endgame is an adventure, science fiction and action film from 2019, directed by Joe Russo. It runs 3h 01m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Marvel Studios, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Mark Ruffalo, Chris Hemsworth. The screenplay was written by Stephen McFeely, Christopher Markus. Reported worldwide gross stands at $2942.2M.
