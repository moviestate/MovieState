The story

What The Dark Knight is about

Batman raises the stakes in his war on crime. With the help of Lt. Jim Gordon and District Attorney Harvey Dent, Batman sets out to dismantle the remaining criminal organizations that plague the streets. The partnership proves to be effective, but they soon find themselves prey to a reign of chaos unleashed by a rising criminal mastermind known to the terrified citizens of Gotham as the Joker.

The Dark Knight is an action, thriller and crime film from 2008, directed by Christopher Nolan. It runs 2h 32m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by Warner Bros. Pictures, Legendary Pictures and Syncopy, with production in United Kingdom, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Christian Bale, Heath Ledger, Aaron Eckhart, Michael Caine. The screenplay was written by Christopher Nolan, David S. Goyer. Reported worldwide gross stands at $1008.5M.
