The story

What Young Hearts is about

14-year-old Elias increasingly feels like an outsider in his village. When he meets his new neighbour of the same age, Alexander, Elias is confronted with his burgeoning sexuality.

Young Hearts is a drama and romance film from 2024, directed by Anthony Schatteman. It runs 1h 39m and is rated NR in the US. It was produced by Polar Bear Films, Family Affair Films and Kwassa Films, with production in Belgium, Netherlands.

The main cast is led by Lou Goossens, Marius De Saeger, Geert Van Rampelberg, Emilie De Roo. The screenplay was written by Anthony Schatteman. Reported worldwide gross stands at $136,344.
