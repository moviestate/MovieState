The story

What Howl's Moving Castle is about

Sophie, a young milliner, is turned into an elderly woman by a witch who enters her shop and curses her. She encounters a wizard named Howl and gets caught up in his resistance to fighting for the king.

Howl's Moving Castle is a fantasy, animation and adventure film from 2004, directed by Hayao Miyazaki. It runs 1h 59m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Studio Ghibli, Tokuma Shoten and Nippon Television Network Corporation, with production in Japan.

The main cast is led by Chieko Baisho, Takuya Kimura, Akihiro Miwa, Tatsuya Gashuin. The screenplay was written by Hayao Miyazaki. Reported worldwide gross stands at $236.0M.
