What Resident Evil is about

Medical courier Bryan unwittingly finds himself fighting for survival as one fateful, horrifying night collapses around him in chaos.

Resident Evil is a horror, science fiction and adventure film from 2026, directed by Zach Cregger. It runs 1h 35m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Constantin Film, Subconscious and Vertigo Entertainment, with production in United States of America, Germany, France.

The main cast is led by Austin Abrams, Paul Walter Hauser, Kali Reis, Zach Cherry. The screenplay was written by Shay Hatten, Zach Cregger. Reported worldwide gross stands at $385.9M.
