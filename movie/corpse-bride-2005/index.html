The story

What Corpse Bride is about

In a 19th-century European village, a young man about to be married is whisked away to the underworld and wed to a mysterious corpse bride, while his real bride waits bereft in the land of the living.

Corpse Bride is a romance, fantasy and animation film from 2005, directed by Mike Johnson. It runs 1h 17m and is rated PG in the US. It was produced by Tim Burton Productions, Patalex Productions and Warner Bros. Pictures, with production in United Kingdom, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Johnny Depp, Helena Bonham Carter, Emily Watson, Tracey Ullman. The screenplay was written by Pamela Pettler, John August. Reported worldwide gross stands at $118.1M.
