What The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers is about

Frodo Baggins and the other members of the Fellowship continue on their sacred quest to destroy the One Ring--but on separate paths. Their destinies lie at two towers - Orthanc Tower in Isengard, where the corrupt wizard Saruman awaits, and Sauron's fortress at Barad-dur, deep within the dark lands of Mordor.

The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers is an adventure, fantasy and action film from 2002, directed by Peter Jackson. It runs 2h 59m and is rated PG-13 in the US. It was produced by New Line Cinema, WingNut Films and The Saul Zaentz Company, with production in New Zealand, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Elijah Wood, Ian McKellen, Viggo Mortensen, Sean Astin. The screenplay was written by Stephen Sinclair, Philippa Boyens. Reported worldwide gross stands at $926.3M.
