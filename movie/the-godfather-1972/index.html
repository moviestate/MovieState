The story

What The Godfather is about

Spanning the years 1945 to 1955, a chronicle of the fictional Italian-American Corleone crime family. When organized crime family patriarch, Vito Corleone barely survives an attempt on his life, his youngest son, Michael steps in to take care of the would-be killers, launching a campaign of bloody revenge.

The Godfather is a drama and crime film from 1972, directed by Francis Ford Coppola. It runs 2h 55m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Paramount Pictures, Alfran Productions and Albert S. Ruddy Productions, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Marlon Brando, Al Pacino, James Caan, Robert Duvall. The screenplay was written by Mario Puzo, Francis Ford Coppola. Reported worldwide gross stands at $245.1M.
