The story

What The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring is about

Young hobbit Frodo Baggins, after inheriting a mysterious ring from his uncle Bilbo, must leave his home in order to keep it from falling into the hands of its evil creator. Along the way, a fellowship is formed to protect the ringbearer and make sure that the ring arrives at its final destination: Mt. Doom, the only place where it can be destroyed.

The Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring is an adventure, fantasy and action film from 2001, directed by Peter Jackson. It runs 2h 59m. It was produced by New Line Cinema, WingNut Films and The Saul Zaentz Company, with production in New Zealand, United States of America.

The main cast is led by Elijah Wood, Ian McKellen, Viggo Mortensen, Sean Astin. The screenplay was written by Philippa Boyens, Fran Walsh. Reported worldwide gross stands at $871.4M.
