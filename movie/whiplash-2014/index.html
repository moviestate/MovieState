The story

What Whiplash is about

Under the direction of a ruthless instructor, a talented young drummer begins to pursue perfection at any cost, even his humanity.

Whiplash is a drama, music and thriller film from 2014, directed by Damien Chazelle. It runs 1h 47m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Bold Films, Blumhouse Productions and Right of Way Films, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by Miles Teller, J.K. Simmons, Paul Reiser, Melissa Benoist. The screenplay was written by Damien Chazelle. Reported worldwide gross stands at $50.3M.
