The story

What Pulp Fiction is about

A burger-loving hit man, his philosophical partner, a drug-addled gangster's moll and a washed-up boxer converge in this sprawling, comedic crime caper. Their adventures unfurl in three stories that ingeniously trip back and forth in time.

Pulp Fiction is a thriller, crime and comedy film from 1994, directed by Quentin Tarantino. It runs 2h 34m and is rated R in the US. It was produced by Miramax, A Band Apart and Jersey Films, with production in United States of America.

The main cast is led by John Travolta, Samuel L. Jackson, Uma Thurman, Bruce Willis. The screenplay was written by Quentin Tarantino, Roger Avary. Reported worldwide gross stands at $213.9M.
