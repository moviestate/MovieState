Where to watch

How to find the right stream in under a minute

  1. Open the film's guide. Every page lists streaming, rental and purchase options reported for the US, UK, Canada, Australia, India and Bangladesh.
  2. Check your region. Rights change per country, so the same film can be included with one service and pay-per-view on another.
  3. Confirm quality and price on the provider. We link out to the service; the final price, HD/4K availability and subtitle tracks are set there.

How we work

Editorial standards, not scraped trivia

Data with a source

Titles, cast, crew, release dates and box office come from TMDB and are re-checked every time a page is rebuilt. When a figure is not reported, we say so instead of guessing.

Availability we verify

Watch options are grouped by country because streaming rights are regional. Each film page names the services reported for that market and links you to the provider to confirm price.

Written for decisions

Every guide answers three questions in order: what the film is, whether it fits your taste, and how to watch it. No autoplay, no pop-ups, no fake download buttons.

FAQ

Questions people ask before pressing play

Is MovieState free to use?

Yes. Every guide, cast list and availability table on this site is free to read. Watching happens on our main site, where each title has its own page.

Do you host or stream the movies?

No. We publish information: story, cast, crew, ratings, release dates and where a film is available. Streaming and downloads are handled on MovieState.

How often is this site updated?

Pages are rebuilt daily from live movie data, so ratings, box office figures and streaming listings change as the sources change.

What is MovieState?

MovieState is an independent movie guide. We research films — story, cast, crew, runtime, release details and availability — and publish clear, fact-checked pages so you can decide what to watch next.

How do I find where to watch a movie?

Open any movie page and use the Watch on MovieState button. Availability changes by country and provider, so always confirm on the streaming page before you subscribe.

Is browsing MovieState free?

Yes. Every page on this site is free to read, with no account and no paywall. We do not run pop-ups or forced sign-ups.

Where does the movie data come from?

Posters, backdrops and core metadata come from TMDB. We combine that with editorial checks against official studio and streaming announcements before publishing.

Do you host or stream movie files?

No. This site publishes information only. Playback and downloads are handled on our main site, moviestate.is-best.net.
