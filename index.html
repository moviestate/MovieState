Frequently asked questions

What is MovieState?

MovieState is an independent movie discovery site. For each film we publish a detail guide — story, cast, runtime, ratings and where to watch — and link you to the MovieState streaming library when you are ready to watch.

How do I find where to watch a movie?

Open any movie guide and read the where-to-watch section. Streaming availability changes by country and provider, so we point you to the library that keeps availability current instead of publishing a list that goes stale.

Is browsing MovieState free?

Yes. Every guide on this site is free to read, with no account and no paywall. Watching happens on the MovieState streaming site.

How current is the information?

Movie facts come from TMDB and are reviewed by our editors. Ratings and availability move constantly, so each guide shows its last update date — treat it as a snapshot, not a promise.

Can I request a movie guide?

Yes. Tell us the title and we will queue it for a guide. Popular requests are prioritised in the publishing calendar.
